An essay in quiet

The Art of Silence

“Silence is a source of great strength.”

— LAO TZU

I

Stillness

Silence is not absence. It is the ground from which every sound rises and to which it returns.

II

Listening

To be quiet is to make room. In that room, others — and the world — can finally speak.

III

The Pause

Music lives in its rests. A conversation breathes in the moment no one fills.

IV

Practice

Begin with one minute. Close your eyes. Notice what remains when you stop adding.

Your breath already holds these lessons. The hold between breaths is stillness. A slow exhale makes room, the way listening does. And the gap before the next inhale is the rest in the music.
